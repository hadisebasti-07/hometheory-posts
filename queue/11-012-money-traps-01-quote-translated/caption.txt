Your reno quote isn't lying. It's just not telling you everything 🧾

6 common quote lines, translated:
1️⃣ "Full-home package" = one lump sum you can't check
2️⃣ "Haulage excluded" = often $1,000+, added mid-project
3️⃣ "Permits & protection: TBC" = $900–2,200
4️⃣ "Hacking included" = the engineer's endorsement isn't: $800–2,000
5️⃣ "Changes later? Can, no problem." = 1.5–2× the price
6️⃣ "50% deposit to start" = red flag. 10–20% upfront at most

Get every line in writing, with measurements, before you sign.
