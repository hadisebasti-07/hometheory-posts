Spot the mistake in this 2-room Flexi 👀

36 sqm, two people working from home… and both desks went into the bedroom. The chairs end up just 50 cm from the bed.

The fix: split the office.
→ Desk 1 moves to the living-room window
→ The dining table doubles as desk 2
Now the bedroom has about 1 m clear again.

📏 Rule of thumb: leave around 90 cm behind a desk chair. A design guideline, not an HDB rule. Measure your own room.
