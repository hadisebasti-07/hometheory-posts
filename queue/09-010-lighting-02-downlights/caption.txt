Hot take: stop adding more downlights 💡❌

A ceiling full of downlights lights everything evenly. That's how offices are lit. Cosy rooms do the opposite: fewer lights overhead, more light down low.

✅ Keep about 4 downlights for cleaning and chores
✅ Add 2 lamps at sitting height: by the sofa and the dining table
✅ Wash one wall with light, like the TV wall
✅ Ask your electrician for dimmable downlights

Fewer holes in your ceiling, and a home that actually feels like home. Agree or disagree?
