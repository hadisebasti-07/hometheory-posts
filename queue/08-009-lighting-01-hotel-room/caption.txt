Your bedroom isn't ugly. Your lighting is 💡

Hotels don't rely on one bright ceiling light. They layer it:
1️⃣ Go warm: bulbs around 2700–3000K
2️⃣ Bring light to eye level: a lamp on each side of the bed
3️⃣ Hide an LED strip behind the headboard: glow, not glare
4️⃣ Light the walls, not just the floor
5️⃣ Put the main light on a dimmer (and use dimmable bulbs)

🔌 Plug-in lamps and LED strips need no rewiring. New wired lighting points must be done by a licensed electrical worker (HDB guidelines).
