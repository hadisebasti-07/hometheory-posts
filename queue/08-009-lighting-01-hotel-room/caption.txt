Why do you look better in hotel mirrors? It's not the holiday 🪞

At home, one ceiling light shines straight down and puts shadows under your eyes. Hotels light your face from the side.

1️⃣ Warm bulbs only: around 2700–3000K. Cool white is for offices.
2️⃣ A lamp on each side of the bed or mirror. Plug-in lamps are enough.
3️⃣ After dinner, the ceiling light goes off.
4️⃣ Get a dimmer, or every night is interrogation mode.

You don't need a holiday. You need two lamps.
