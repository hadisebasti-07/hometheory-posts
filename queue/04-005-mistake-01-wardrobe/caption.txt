Spot the $ mistake in this bedroom 👀

The wardrobe runs the whole wall, all ~11 ft. But the bedroom door swings into the last ~3 ft, so the door can't open fully and that corner is a pain to use.

Wardrobes are quoted per foot: about $250–400 per foot for sliding laminate. Those ~3 ft cost roughly $750–1,200.

The fix: stop the wardrobe before the door swing. ~8 ft of wardrobe, a door that opens, money saved.

📏 Rule of thumb: draw every door swing on the plan before you sign the carpentry drawing.
