That 11.11 sofa might not fit through your door. Measure these 4 things first 📏

1️⃣ The wall space it'll sit against, and the item's full width (arms included)
2️⃣ The walkway left over: aim for around 90 cm (a design guideline, not an HDB rule)
3️⃣ Will it fit through your door and lift on delivery day?
4️⃣ What it'll block: sockets, switches and windows

Write the numbers down, then add to cart. A sale price doesn't help if the sofa won't fit 🛋️
