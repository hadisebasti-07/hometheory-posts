Your ID asks you to pay into their personal account? Stop. 🛑

🏢 Your contract is with the company, not the designer.
💸 Money sent to a personal account may never reach the firm. In 2024, a designer was jailed for taking S$20K+ this way, and another was arrested in Aug 2026 over a similar scheme.
✅ Pay only the company account, in the same name as your contract.
🪜 Pay in stages. 10 / 40 / 40 / 10 is common: signing, hacking & masonry, carpentry installed, handover. Many guides call a deposit over 20% a red flag.
