Hot take: the cheapest reno quote isn't cheaper. It's shorter ✂️

Put 3 quotes side by side and count the lines, not the totals. The cheap one often skips:
❌ Haulage & debris (often $1,000+, added mid-project)
❌ Permits & protection ($900–2,200)
❌ The engineer's endorsement if walls are hacked ($800–2,000)

Those costs don't disappear. One cost guide says a quote far below the others "almost always reappears later as variation orders". And anything added after you sign can cost 1.5–2× more.

✅ Compare line by line. Missing line? Ask for it in writing.
